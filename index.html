02 — Problems I Solve

Where standard execution quietly breaks down.

Disconnected Marketing

You post consistently. But if you stopped tomorrow, what would actually change? You invest in content, but if the execution lacks a direct line to pipeline operations, consistency is wasted.

AI Without Control

You upgraded the tools. But did you actually buy back time, or just create another thing your team has to manage? Ungoverned AI usage creates brand inconsistency, execution rework, and security risks. I build the operational guardrails.

Execution Trapped Inside People

When team knowledge and execution live entirely in an individual's memory rather than documented systems, the business is operating at high risk. If that person leaves, transitions, or is simply unavailable, your entire marketing pipeline stalls. I solve this by default: I build, document, and scale repeatable standard operating procedures (SOPs) so that the business — not the individual — owns the operational process.
